# Market Day

Went into town looking for a birthday present for a friend. We talked through the options together and narrowed it to jewellery, something small and bright.

Settled on a pair of daisy earrings, silver with little enamel petals. They looked like something a forest girl would wear. Wrapped them in green tissue paper at the kitchen table.

A good day. Sunlit, unhurried.
